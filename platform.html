How to use

Hover a node to see its neighbours. Click to pin it and open details. Drag nodes to rearrange, scroll or pinch to zoom, drag the background to pan.

Organization centrality

Ranking of the organizations by degree, betweenness and eigenvector centrality in the current network, so it follows the category filter. Choose whether every tie counts the same (unweighted) or a stronger tie counts more (weighted). Click an organization to open its profile. Organizations without ties in the view are not ranked; clear “Hide organizations without ties” to list them at the end.

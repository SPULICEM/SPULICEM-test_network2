How it works

The graph view shows the network of all actors (nodes) and belief affinities (edges). Everyone has a certain degree of belief affinity.

A belief is measured from the discourse. Each statement made by any actor within 2020–2026 was coded to extract the main belief.

Example quote and coding
"We believe that the focus on technology and innovation will provide better economic gains for companies and society."
Main beliefs: Focus on technology Focus on economic orientation

When two organisations share the same beliefs, then they are tied.

By applying this process to all 124 organisations, we can now explore the communities formed around shared beliefs.

Each community has its own profile of similar beliefs related to its internal values, mission, and daily policy actions.

We can now discover not only the organisations that we already collaborate with, but new and surprising potential partners that think in line with what you also think.

THIS IS THE BASIS FOR NEW COLLABORATION; IDEAS MATTER AND SUSTAIN BOTH IMPLEMENTATION AS WELL AS NEW POLICY DESIGNS.

Welcome to the project ‘Exploring the governance arrangements to smart, resilient and sustainable cities in Brazil’

Here, we aim to provide information about governance arrangements that could strengthen smart, resilient and sustainable city governance.

You will discover potential groups, policy actors, and discourses that enhance governance efficiency and effectiveness in these agendas.

About the project

This is part of the CCD: Carbon Neutral Cities project — postdoctoral project grants 2025/13377-2 and 2026/12936-0, named “Arranjos de governança para cidades inteligentes, resilientes e sustentáveis: subsídios a partir das análises de discursos e de redes” and “Análise avançada em redes e estruturas de governança para cidades inteligentes, resilientes e sustentáveis” — held by the technological research institute in São Paulo and the National Centre for early warning and monitoring of natural hazards, funded by FAPESP, and in collaboration with Edinburgh Napier University.
